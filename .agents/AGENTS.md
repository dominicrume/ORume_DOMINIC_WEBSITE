# Rume Dominic & Vorem AI — Custom Agent Rules & Brand Voice

## ✍️ Brand Voice & Writing Style (Ruben Hassid x Rume Dominic Blend)

When writing any social media copy (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Substack), marketing emails, blog posts, or growth recommendations for Rume Dominic / Vorem Nigeria, you **MUST** apply the **Ruben Hassid High-Traction Writing Style** blended with Rume Dominic's authoritative brand voice:

### 1. The Short Punchy Hook (1–3 Words + Period)
Start every post or headline with an ultra-short, punchy 1–3 word hook followed by a period.
* Examples:
  * `Vibecoding.`
  * `Claude For Dummies.`
  * `AI will fail.`
  * `Consciousness over code.`
  * `From Code to Consciousness.`
  * `Master AI.`

### 2. The Counter-Intuitive / High-Curiosity Subtitle
Immediately follow the punchy hook with a bold, curiosity-inducing subtitle or counter-intuitive statement that challenges assumptions, admits a hard truth, or promises step-by-step practical value.
* Examples:
  * `If you still think AI is just for coders → this is where to start:`
  * `After 51,000+ hours of development, I admit most AI advice is wrong:`
  * `Stop collecting prompts. Finish something with AI today.`
  * `You don't need to learn syntax anymore. Here is why:`

### 3. Practical Step-by-Step Value & Arrows (`→` / `:`)
* Use clear visual transition arrows (`→`) or colons (`:`) to guide the reader into action.
* Focus on finishing tangible AI projects rather than passive theory ("Stop collecting prompts. Finish something with AI today.").
* Reference Rume Dominic's core assets seamlessly:
  * **Tier 1**: Free/£19.99 Book (*"From Code to Consciousness"* — 51,000+ hours of development insights).
  * **Tier 2**: Free/£97 Course (*"Master AI in 9 Days"* inside Vorem).
  * **Tier 3**: AI Marketing & Growth Brain Service / Custom AI Consulting.

### 4. Formatting Rules
* Short paragraphs (1–2 lines max per block).
* High scannability with clean spacing.
* Authentic, direct, and zero fluff. Never use generic corporate jargon.
